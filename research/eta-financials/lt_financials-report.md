# Lithuanian ETA — Lithuanian target financials (public-source dataset)

Research cut-off: 2026-07-28 UTC.  **25 named Lithuanian companies**.  No outreach was performed.

## Method and limits

- Sources are free public Rekvizitai company pages, their annual financial pages and their Sodra-backed insured-employee history pages. Every company has a 9-digit Lithuanian legal-entity code and at least one reported turnover year.
- Turnover, pre-tax profit and net profit are transcribed from the free annual-financial table when available. These are reported annual figures as displayed by the public source, but they are not a substitute for obtaining signed Registry Centre accounts in diligence.
- Free pages retrieved did not disclose operating profit or D&A consistently. Therefore the numerical EBITDA field is an **estimated screening proxy**, not filed EBITDA: latest reported turnover × 9%; low/high sensitivity is 6%/12%. It must not be used as a valuation conclusion.
- Estimated payroll cost is calculated only where both Sodra-backed employee count and gross monthly pay are displayed: employees × average monthly gross pay × 12 × 1.0177. It is a payroll-cost proxy, not a filed expense.
- Employee trend compares newest and oldest insured-headcount observations on the public history page. A dash means it was not exposed or retrievable.
