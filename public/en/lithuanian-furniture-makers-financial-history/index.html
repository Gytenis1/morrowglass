Method and limitations

What this time series covers and what it cannot establish.

Source and validation boundary

Every figure is calculated at static-site build time from the versioned data/manufacturers.json file and only from its valid filed_financial_history values.

Financial amounts are included only when the specific line filed with the Lithuanian Register of Legal Entities has API evidence in the Lithuanian open-data portal registry financial statement data. Source: Juridinių asmenų registro (Registrų centro) finansinių ataskaitų duomenys Lietuvos atvirų duomenų portale.

How to interpret the results

This is catalogue coverage, not Lithuanian national statistics or furniture-market statistics. The catalogue does not cover every Lithuanian company, and source records use mixed financial-statement templates and fiscal periods.

This page is not a company ranking, endorsement or assessment of work quality, capacity, availability or suitability for a particular project. It cannot establish the size of the whole market or the service quality of an individual company.
