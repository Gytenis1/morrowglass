# Morrowglass Disclaimer and AI Transparency Copy

Use these canonical disclaimers consistently across onboarding, result screens, paid flows, and marketing. Keep the language visible, plain, and close to the relevant action.

## Core Entertainment Disclaimer

> Morrowglass is for entertainment and self-reflection only. Astrology and optional AI face-reading results are symbolic, not factual assessments or predictions. Do not use Morrowglass for medical, financial, legal, relationship, safety, or other important decisions.

## Short Disclaimer

> Entertainment only. Not factual advice or a prediction.

## Onboarding Disclaimer

> Welcome to Morrowglass. Your readings are designed for entertainment, reflection, and playful discovery. They are not scientific, medical, financial, legal, relationship, or safety advice, and they should not be treated as factual predictions.

## Face-Reading Consent Screen Disclaimer

> Optional AI face-reading uses your image to create a playful entertainment reading. It does not identify you, verify you, or infer sensitive traits such as race, ethnicity, political opinions, religion, trade union membership, sex life, sexual orientation, or health. We do not store your raw image, and you can revoke consent and delete face-reading data at any time.

## Result Screen Disclaimer

> This reading is entertainment-only and generated from symbolic astrology inputs and/or optional AI face-reading descriptors. It is not a factual assessment of who you are and should not be used for important decisions.

## Paid Reading Disclaimer

> Paid readings are entertainment content. Purchase does not guarantee accuracy, outcomes, or predictions about health, money, relationships, work, or life events. Use the reading as a playful reflection tool only.

## Checkout/Pre-Purchase Microcopy

> By purchasing, you understand this is entertainment content only and not professional advice or a factual prediction.

## AI Transparency Notices

### Before face upload

> AI transparency notice: if you choose face-reading, Morrowglass uses automated analysis of your image to create an entertainment result. The feature is optional, does not identify you, and does not infer sensitive traits. Your raw image is not stored.

### On result page

> AI-generated entertainment notice: parts of this reading were generated automatically from your selected inputs and, if you consented, non-identifying face descriptors. Treat it as symbolic entertainment, not a factual assessment.

### Settings/privacy page

> Optional face-reading uses automated processing only with your explicit consent. You can revoke consent at any time. Revocation stops future face-reading and deletes stored face-reading descriptors/results.

### Paid flow

> AI transparency notice: your paid reading may include automatically generated entertainment text based on your inputs. It is not professional advice or a factual prediction.

## Copy Rules

Approved tone:

- “symbolic”
- “playful”
- “for entertainment”
- “may suggest”
- “reflection prompt”
- “cosmic twin”
- “archetype”

Avoid:

- “proves”
- “diagnoses”
- “predicts with accuracy”
- “guarantees”
- “detects your ethnicity/religion/sexuality/health”
- “will happen”
- “your partner will…”
- “you should invest…”
- “you have a condition…”

## Examples of Safe Marketing Lines

- “Find your playful cosmic twin — entertainment only.”
- “A symbolic mirror for your star chart and optional face-reading archetype.”
- “See which celebrity-style cosmic energy matches your vibe.”
- “Unlock a hidden trait prompt for self-reflection, not prediction.”

## Examples to Reject

- “We predict your soulmate and when you will meet.”
- “Your face reveals your health future.”
- “AI knows whether your relationship will last.”
- “Discover your true ethnicity from your face.”
- “This reading tells you what financial move to make.”
