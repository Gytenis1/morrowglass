# Morrowglass Launch Marketing Asset Kit

This kit contains ready-to-use launch copy for the first Morrowglass short-form campaign.

## Contents

- `script-1-celebrity-cosmic-twin.md` — short-form video script and captions for the celebrity-style cosmic twin hook.
- `script-2-two-friends.md` — short-form video script and captions for two friends comparing archetypes.
- `script-3-hidden-trait.md` — short-form video script and captions for the share-to-unlock mechanic.
- `script-4-founder-challenge.md` — short-form video script and captions for the 7-day launch experiment.
- `script-5-result-roulette.md` — short-form video script and captions for the archetype roulette format.
- `x-launch-thread.md` — launch announcement thread for X.

## How to use

1. Replace only `[APP_URL]` with the live app link before posting.
2. Keep the footer at the end of every caption or post.
3. Keep visuals aligned with consent and privacy rules: do not show user images, birth time/place, or private details unless the user has clearly agreed.
4. Use these assets as playful entertainment copy; do not add claims beyond the approved language below.

## Compliance rule

Every post must include the entertainment-only disclaimer. Do not make health, finance, legal, relationship, safety, or factual-prediction claims. Do not say that AI identifies sensitive traits. Do not claim results are guaranteed, scientific, or suitable for important decisions.

Approved tone words and phrases:

- symbolic
- playful
- entertainment
- archetype
- cosmic twin
- reflection
- Find your playful cosmic twin.
- Unlock a symbolic hidden trait.
- Compare archetypes with friends.
- For entertainment and self-reflection.

Do not use these claims or phrasing:

- “Predict your soulmate.”
- “Find out if your relationship will last.”
- “Your face reveals your health future.”
- “This reading tells you what job or investment to choose.”
- “AI detects your ethnicity, sexuality, religion, or mental health.”
- “proves”
- “diagnoses”
- “predicts with accuracy”
- “guarantees”
- “will happen”
- “your partner will…”
- “you should invest…”
- “you have a condition…”

Use these canonical disclaimers consistently:

> Morrowglass is for entertainment and self-reflection only. Astrology and optional AI face-reading results are symbolic, not factual assessments or predictions. Do not use Morrowglass for medical, financial, legal, relationship, safety, or other important decisions.

Short social footer:

> Entertainment only — not advice, diagnosis, or a factual prediction.
