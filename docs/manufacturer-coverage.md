# Manufacturer static-catalogue coverage

Coverage snapshot: 450 versioned `manufacturers` records. `data/manufacturers.json` has 450 unique slugs: the prior 315-record catalogue plus 135 distinct legal-entity candidates in `data/official_furniture_candidates_20260801.json`. The new batch is sourced on 2026-08-01 from the free official [Lithuanian Open Data Portal dataset 2088](https://data.gov.lt/datasets/2088/) (legal-entity economic activities; Registrų centras / Valstybės duomenų agentūra provenance). Every appended profile carries its own inspectable `get.data.gov.lt` record URL, legal name, company code, and the official EVRK 2.1 activity `Baldų gamyba`.

**Conservative classification and limitations:** dataset 2088 verifies legal identity, active status, and broad furniture manufacturing only. It does not provide a product specialty, customer audience, portfolio, contact data, employee data, revenue, or a city for this batch. The appended records therefore use category **O / Kiti nestandartiniai baldai**, `vidutinis` confidence, `nežinoma` audience/portfolio status, and no financial or contact enrichment. `Lietuva` and the `national` region label mean country-level source coverage only; they do **not** claim a city or service territory. The static catalogue retains a required city field for route generation, so it displays this country-level location transparently as `Lietuva` rather than inventing a municipality.

**Identity deduplication:** all 450 records are checked by slug, populated company code, and normalized published name. Two redundant legacy directory aliases were replaced in place while retaining the higher-quality existing profiles for their legal entities. The replacements are [UAB "GM ART" (code 306083828)](https://get.data.gov.lt/datasets/gov/lsd/cl/ja_asmenys/JuridinisAsmuo/1a0c2d53-0e8b-473a-b63c-a0a997f9cea3) and [Uždaroji akcinė bendrovė "FENOLITA" (code 124264828)](https://get.data.gov.lt/datasets/gov/lsd/cl/ja_asmenys/JuridinisAsmuo/1a197d41-7008-4e59-8881-28b9ef9ffc20). Their direct official entries identify active legal entities and EVRK 2.1 activity `Baldų gamyba`; because the source does not provide specialty, contact, employee, owner, or revenue facts, their descriptions and classifications remain conservative. These two replacements occupy legacy positions, so the immutable 135-record `official_furniture_candidates_20260801.json` batch remains unchanged and continues to match the appended source segment exactly.

The static generator rejects a missing, malformed, or duplicate profile slug; a missing publishable name/city; malformed category inputs; duplicate generated routes; duplicate category/city routes; and duplicate sitemap paths. `scripts/import-manufacturers.mjs --validate` requires exactly 450 source records, validates all new official-source metadata and conservative classifications, and rejects every populated duplicate company code. Any future unpublishable live record must be recorded here with its slug/record identifier and concrete reason rather than being omitted.
